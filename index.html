Why Chesapeake

01

Serve Your Community

From quiet farmland to busy neighborhoods and waterways, Chesapeake is a city of communities. Our officers coach youth teams, know the business owners on their beat, and show up long before there's an emergency. That everyday trust is what keeps Chesapeake safe.

Specialty Units

Find Your
Mission

Every officer starts in Patrol. From there, Chesapeake offers opportunities to specialize in the work that drives you.

Patrol Operations

The backbone of the department. Patrol officers are first on scene, answering calls and working proactively across every part of the city.

Investigations

Detectives follow cases from the crime scene to the courtroom, bringing answers to victims and accountability to offenders.

Vice & Narcotics

Targeting drug distribution and vice crimes that harm neighborhoods, using surveillance and long-term investigations.

Gang Unit

Identifying, disrupting, and preventing gang activity through enforcement, intelligence, and community partnerships.

SWAT

Highly trained officers who handle the department's highest-risk incidents with precision, teamwork, and specialized equipment.

Marine Patrol & Maritime Operations

Protecting Chesapeake's waterways, including the Intracoastal Waterway and the Elizabeth River, with boat patrols, rescues, and enforcement.

School Resource Officer

Keeping students and staff safe while serving as a mentor and trusted adult in Chesapeake's schools.

Drone Team

Officers trained as drone pilots give responders an eye in the sky for searches, crash scenes, and major incidents across the city.

What is the starting salary?

Police Officers start at $59,000 after graduating from the academy, with raises through a competitive step-pay plan.

When does the next academy start?

The next police academy begins March 1, 2027. Apply early. The hiring process takes time to complete.

Do officers get a take-home car?

Yes. Officers who live in the City of Chesapeake are eligible for a take-home vehicle after three years of service.

How do I apply?

Complete the online application at jobs.cityofchesapeake.net. Our recruiter is happy to help if you have questions along the way.

I'm in the military. Can I start the process before I separate?

Yes. CPD participates in the DoD SkillBridge program, which lets eligible transitioning service members train with the department during their final months of service. Contact our recruiter to learn more.

What are the minimum qualifications?
  • Be a United States citizen
  • Be at least 19 and a half years old at the time of application
  • Be 20 years of age at the time of graduation from the academy
  • Be a high school graduate or possess a GED/HSE
  • Possess a valid motor vehicle operator's license with an acceptable driving record in accordance with City Driving Standards
Does the police department have a tattoo policy?

Tattoos, brands, and body art located on the head, face, neck, or scalp (including ears, lips, and eyelids) are prohibited. Cosmetic tattoos (such as eyeliner, eyebrows, and lips) are permitted only if they are natural in appearance.

What are the automatic disqualifiers?

Disqualifiers are based on state law, department and city policies, and special certification restrictions.

  • More than two convictions involving any moving violation within the last 12 months.
  • Convictions that indicate the valid driver's license is/was denied, suspended, or revoked during the past two years for any reason other than failure to pay fines or other non-drug or alcohol-related administrative reason(s).
  • Drug or alcohol-related driving convictions within the past three years.
  • More than one conviction of reckless driving or driving 20 or more miles over the speed limit within the past three years.
  • Convictions that are so egregious that they could be deemed negligent in the City's duties to the public may be addressed separately through the City's Disciplinary Policy or may result in disqualification of an applicant, intern, or volunteer. The date of conviction and anticipated date of hire will be used when determining eligibility under this policy.
  • Convicted of, plead guilty to, or no contest to any felony.
  • Convicted of, plead guilty to, or no contest to a misdemeanor involving acts of moral turpitude (i.e. lying, cheating, and stealing).
  • Convicted of, plead guilty to, or no contest to a misdemeanor sex offense.
  • Convicted of, plead guilty to, or no contest to domestic assault.
  • Conviction of possession, manufacture, and/or distribution of illegal drugs, excluding possession of marijuana or any cannabis derivative.
  • Use of Cocaine, LSD, PCP, or any type of hallucinogenic drug in the last five years. (Subject to ADA guidelines)
  • Dishonorable discharge, Bad conduct/Misconduct discharge, and Other than Honorable discharges from military service. General and Uncharacterized discharges from military service will be evaluated on a case-by-case basis.
  • Untruthfulness or the withholding of pertinent information during any phase of the application process (e.g. application, interview, paperwork etc.).
  • Cheating on any examination or testing associated with the process.
What specialty units can I join?

After gaining experience in Patrol, officers can pursue assignments including Investigations, Vice & Narcotics, the Gang Unit, SWAT, Marine Patrol and Maritime Operations, School Resource Officer, and more.
